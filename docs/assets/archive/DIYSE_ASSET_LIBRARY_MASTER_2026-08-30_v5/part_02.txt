## 8. Environment Archetypes We Can Already Support

- Forested stone ruins, wetland/forest areas, giant-tree/root spaces and overgrown masonry.
- Stone fortresses, castles, towers, chapels/cathedral-like halls and defensive interiors.
- Dark/firelit, ceremonial, magical, high-status and richly furnished manor/bedroom interiors.
- Timber/half-timbered towns, rural villages, courtyards, workshops and shop-like rooms.
- Harbor/dock/ship-adjacent environments.
- Windmill/farm/rural-mechanical and clockwork/mechanical spaces.
- Water-heavy environments with surfaces, sides, waves, ripples and splashes.
- Cold/snow/ice-oriented environments.
- Desert/sandy/cactus environments.
- Volcanic/lava environments with animated terrain and bubbling effects.
- A substantial cave/cavern/subterranean environment family with multiple rock and moss treatments.
- Ritual/magic-circle ceremonial spaces and arcane floor motifs.
- Alternate-state or directional-light environments using A/B/C, numbered or `ra`–`rf` lighting sets.

These are **asset-support statements**, not locked DIYSE map designs. Original DIYSE layouts remain gameplay-first and should combine these assets with original project-specific work.
