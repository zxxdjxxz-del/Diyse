### Core production conclusion

The extracted map files are **not complete playable maps**. They are a raw environment-asset library: atlases, lighting passes, transparent scenery, shadows, water/effect frames, doors, props, alternate visual states and animated environment textures. DIYSE layouts, collision, paths, encounters, treasure, elevation, camera and map composition remain original project work.

The Quaternius Fantasy Props MegaKit and both Universal Animation Libraries remain verified **CC0 1.0 / Public Domain Dedication**.

## 4. Technical Findings From Direct Texture Inspection

### Earlier foundation
- The source uses environment color atlases plus matching lighting data, separate alpha-cut scenery, shadows, water/effect sequences, object textures and reusable state variants.
- Modular reuse has repeatedly been confirmed across different map numbers.

### Maps096–116 verification
- **184/184 TGAs decode successfully**.
- Mode split: **149 RGB + 35 RGBA**.
- Most common sizes: **256×256 (97)**, **512×512 (41)**, **2048×2048 (35)**, **1024×1024 (8)**, **128×128 (2)**, **512×256 (1)**.
- Exact-unique within this batch: **184**.
- Newly unique relative to v4: **183**.
- Missing from the 096–116 range: **097, 098, 099, 101, 102**.

### New rendering / asset patterns
- **Map096:** highly furnished ornate blue/brown interior; separate chair and right-side object/component.
- **Map100:** green courtyard/ruin family with object, shadow and two numbered lighting states for both map and object.
- **Map103:** bright cliff/stone/windmill environment with a separate windmill and **30 numbered windmill-lighting states**.
- **Map104:** richly furnished manor/bedroom interior with floor, curtain, window, bed, flower, open/closed doors, multiple secondary lighting passes and two **15-state light-shaft lighting systems**.
- **Maps105–114:** a broad cave/cavern construction family spanning pale rock, mossy green stone, stalactite/stalagmite textures and several distinct subterranean visual treatments.
- **Map111:** complete fresh water package with **30-frame surface**, **8-frame wave**, and **two separate 8-frame splash systems**.
- **Map115:** outdoor stone/ruin approach family; its shadow is an exact reuse of the Map038 shadow.
- **Map116:** distinctive large ritual/magic-circle environment atlas suitable as reference/source material for ceremonial or arcane floor motifs.

## 5. Reusable Asset-System Categories Confirmed

- **Architecture / terrain:** stone, timber/plaster, ruins, castles, towers, ceremonial halls, furnished manor interiors, mechanical interiors, cold/snow, volcanic/lava, desert/sandy environments, giant-tree/root spaces and a broad cave/cavern family.
- **Vegetation:** multiple tree families, icon trees, cactus/icon-cactus, animated grass/reeds, trunk/root pieces, flowers and overgrown-ground families.
- **Water:** numerous 30-frame and shorter surface loops, sides, waves, ripples and splash families.
- **Lighting:** standard atlases, A/B/C states, numbered lighting sequences, six-way `ra`–`rf` directional/rotation lighting families, 30-state windmill lighting and 15-state light-shaft systems.
- **Doors / gates:** many 5-frame door families, open/closed states, front/back and door-space pieces.
- **Fire / candles:** fire, fireplace and multiple candle/candlestick animation families.
- **Magic / atmosphere:** magic squares, ritual-circle imagery, symbols, long light shafts, glare and translucent effects.
- **Mechanical scenery:** propellers, windmills, bells, gears and moving-light state assets.
- **Interior dressing:** beds, chairs, curtains, flowers, tables, boards, weapons, cloth, furniture and miscellaneous objects.
- **Volcanic effects:** animated lava/environment states and bubbling effects.
- **Shadows:** separate alpha-ready ground/object shadows throughout the library.
