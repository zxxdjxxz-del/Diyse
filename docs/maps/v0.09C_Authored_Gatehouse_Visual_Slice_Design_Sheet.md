## 1. Scope and Canon Status

v0.09C replaces runtime-painted calibration art for three accepted gatehouse cameras with reproducible authored visual assets:

1. stair court;
2. lower arch passage;
3. upper bridge.

The accepted v0.09B field geometry, elevation, movement, camera definitions, interaction anchors, mechanism state, hazard timing, summit route, shortcut, battle integration, save schema, and signing identity remain unchanged.

The visual pipeline, layer separation, file contracts, alignment rules, bridge-state presentation, and shared Cyanis sheet are implementation-authoritative for this prototype. Final gatehouse placement, architecture, materials, exact historical appearance, exact Crest depiction, final Cyanis model, environmental audio, encounters, rewards, and production-quality rendering remain provisional.

---

## 2. Location Identity and Three Visible Time Layers

The slice retains the provisional old Yahtrean gatehouse identity established by v0.09B:

- **Original function:** a defended Crest-operated passage with layered stairs, lower service access, and a controlled upper bridge.
- **Transformation:** structural damage and abandonment disconnect the bridge and expose lower routes.
- **Current adaptation:** Cyanis uses the surviving mechanism to restore temporary upper access.

The authored plates must make these layers more legible through masonry age, damaged connections, later bracing, inactive machinery, route lighting, and the visible difference between lower service circulation and upper defensive circulation.

## 4. Composition Plan

| Camera | Player purpose | Authored layers | Dynamic state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stair court | Read the lower court, diagonal stairs, and upper destination | background, atmosphere, foreground | none |
| Lower arch | Pass beneath the upper route while retaining Cyanis visibility | background, atmosphere, arch foreground mask | selective fade and silhouette |

The remaining approach, mechanism, pulse, and summit cameras retain the v0.09B procedural plates during this focused slice.

## 5. Movement and Geometry Preservation

The overlay must not modify `GatehouseMap.java` or any accepted route coordinates.

The following remain fixed:

- camera-relative analog movement;
- acceleration and deceleration;
- wall sliding;
- diagonal stair slope;
- elevation continuity;
- camera-basis blending;
- edge protection;
- route connection widths;
- mechanism interaction zone;
- bridge navigation state;
- Ruin-pulse timing and checkpoints;
- summit shortcut;
- Cyanis-only normal field presentation;
- no permanent follower train.

The authored plates are aligned to the existing affine camera projection rather than changing movement to fit the art.

## 6. Asset Contract

### Field plates

- 1280 × 720 RGBA PNG;
- separate background, atmosphere, and foreground files;
- transparent pixels preserved in atmosphere and foreground layers;
- exact filenames documented in the runtime visual manifest;
- deterministic output from the checked-in offline generator;
- linear filtering at runtime;
- disposed with the owning screen.

### Upper bridge state

The inactive background depicts the disconnected route. `upper_bridge_active.png` is drawn only after the existing mechanism activates the navigation connection.

### Cyanis sheet

- one 640 × 896 RGBA PNG;
- four columns and four rows;
- each frame 160 × 224;
- rows: Down, Up, Left, Right;
- column zero idle;
- columns one through three walking;
- stable foot anchor across frames;
- strong silhouette at the v0.09B near and far scale ranges.

---

## 7. Runtime Layer Order

1. background plate;
2. activated bridge overlay when applicable;
3. authored atmosphere;
4. existing real-time atmospheric particles and Ruin effects;
5. Cyanis grounding shadow;
6. Cyanis field frame;
7. foreground mask;
8. selective Cyanis silhouette when the lower arch significantly obscures him;
9. UI and transition fade.

Camera crossfades must support authored-to-authored and authored-to-procedural transitions without leaving a stale atmosphere layer visible.

---

## 8. Android Performance and Fallback

The first slice loads authored assets for three of seven cameras. The other four continue using lightweight procedural plates.

Device acceptance will determine whether production needs:

- 960 × 540 plate variants;
- 640 × 360 low-memory variants;
- deferred loading by camera group;
- texture-atlas consolidation;
- compressed texture formats.

The shared Cyanis sheet replaces sixteen separately generated runtime textures and should reduce field-character allocation overhead.
